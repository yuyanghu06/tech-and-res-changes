# Mod DLC-style presentation

`dlc_ztr/` contains the mod's DLC-style descriptor, JSON presentation metadata and its own thumbnail. These are separate from local-mod identity under `.metadata` and from paid-game-pack conditions such as `ip5_cosmetics`.

See [interface, localization and metadata](../docs/visuals/interface-localization-and-metadata.md). Preserve established descriptor names, IDs and relative artwork paths; do not add a paid-pack dependency based on a filename.
