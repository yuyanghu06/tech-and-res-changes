# Events

Events implement country paths, population changes, research outcomes, diplomacy, climate disasters and other triggered changes. A defined event is not proof it has an active caller. Trace its namespace/identifier from journal entries, decisions, recurring callbacks, buttons and other events.

Use [diplomacy and country content](../docs/gameplay/diplomacy-and-country-content.md), [demographics](../docs/gameplay/demographics.md), [military and nuclear systems](../docs/gameplay/military-and-nuclear.md) and [climate](../docs/gameplay/climate-and-environment.md) for the relevant paths. Preserve scope names, saved variables, trigger conditions, cleanup and localization when editing.
