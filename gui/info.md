# Inline icons

The two current `.gui` files define inline icons for goods and other mod concepts. They are not the mod's journal control-panel implementation. Those controls live in journal entries, scripted buttons, scripted GUIs, progress bars and localization under `common` and `localization`.

See [interface, localization and metadata](../docs/visuals/interface-localization-and-metadata.md). Keep icon identifiers and referenced textures consistent with displayed text.
